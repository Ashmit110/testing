         Department of Computer Science & Engineering

OVERVIEW
--------
This document describes the end-to-end procedures for bill forwarding, expense
reimbursement, and vendor payment within the department. It applies to all faculty,
staff, TAs, and research scholars who incur department-approved expenses. Strict
adherence to these workflows ensures timely payments and accurate financial records.

================================================================
SECTION 1: TYPES OF REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
================================================================

Category A — Travel Expenses (Conference/Research):
  - Airfare (economy class only; business class requires Chair pre-approval)
  - Hotel accommodation (maximum $180/night domestic, $250/night international)
  - Ground transportation (taxi, rideshare, public transit with receipts)
  - Meals per diem: $55/day domestic, $75/day international (no receipts required)
  - Conference registration fees (receipt required)
  - Visa application fees for international conferences

Category B — Research & Lab Supplies:
  - Equipment purchases up to $500 (above $500 requires purchase order)
  - Software licenses (one-time or annual, department use only)
  - Printed materials, posters for conferences
  - Lab consumables (reagents, cables, storage media)

Category C — TA-Related Expenses:
  - Course materials for which no department copy exists (must have faculty approval)
  - Printing costs for graded assignments (if departmental printer unavailable)

Non-Reimbursable Items:
  - Personal expenses during travel (entertainment, alcohol, personal phone calls)
  - Expenses incurred without prior approval where approval was required
  - Purchases from non-approved vendors for items over $200
  - Penalties or fees incurred due to late cancellations or personal scheduling

================================================================
SECTION 2: PRE-APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

The following expenses require written pre-approval BEFORE the expense is incurred:

  Expense Type                        Approval Authority       Threshold
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  Domestic conference travel          Graduate Coordinator     Any amount
  International conference travel     Department Chair         Any amount
  Equipment purchase                  Department Chair         > $200
  Software license                    IT Admin + Chair         > $100/year
  Vendor invoice forwarding           Finance Officer          > $500
  Guest speaker honorarium            Department Chair         Any amount

How to Obtain Pre-Approval:
  1. Submit Form FIN-PRE-01 (Pre-Approval Request) via the department portal.
  2. Attach a brief justification and estimated cost breakdown.
  3. Allow 5 business days for review. Urgent requests (travel within 10 days)
     are flagged "URGENT" and reviewed within 48 hours.
  4. Approved request generates a pre-approval code (e.g., PA-2024-0089).
     This code must appear on all reimbursement forms submitted later.

================================================================
SECTION 3: REIMBURSEMENT SUBMISSION WORKFLOW
================================================================

Step 1 — Gather Documentation:
  Collect all original receipts. Scanned/photographed receipts are accepted if
  legible. Credit card statements alone are NOT accepted as proof of payment.
  For per diem meals, no receipts are required but travel dates must be documented.

Step 2 — Complete Reimbursement Form (Form FIN-REIMB-01):
  Fields required:
    - Claimant name, student/employee ID, department
    - Pre-approval code (if applicable)
    - Itemized list of expenses with dates, amounts, and purpose
    - Bank account details for direct deposit (first submission only)
    - Claimant signature and date

Step 3 — Supervisor Sign-Off:
  Faculty claiming their own expenses: signed by Department Chair.
  Students/TAs: signed by supervising faculty member.
  Staff: signed by department administrator.

Step 4 — Submit to Finance Office:
  Physical: Room 115, Administrative Wing (Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 3 PM)
  Digital:  finance.cse@university.edu (PDF only, named as
            [EmployeeID]_REIMB_[YYYYMM].pdf)

Step 5 — Finance Review (3–5 business days):
  Finance officer verifies pre-approval, checks receipts, and confirms budget
  availability. If documents are incomplete, the claimant is notified within 2
  business days with a specific list of missing items.

Step 6 — Payment Processing:
  Approved claims are processed through university payroll.
  Direct deposit: 7–10 business days after finance approval.
  Check (for guests/non-employees): 10–15 business days.

================================================================
SECTION 4: BILL FORWARDING PROCEDURE (VENDOR INVOICES)
================================================================

When the department receives a vendor invoice (e.g., for equipment, services,
or subscriptions), the following forwarding workflow applies:

  Step 1: Receiving staff logs invoice in the Vendor Invoice Register (VIR)
          within 24 hours of receipt. Invoice is assigned a VIR number.
  Step 2: Invoice forwarded to the relevant faculty/project PI via email with
          VIR number in subject. PI must confirm receipt within 2 business days.
  Step 3: PI reviews the invoice against the purchase order (PO) or agreement.
          If discrepancy exists, PI contacts vendor directly and informs Finance.
  Step 4: PI returns the invoice with written approval (signature on printed copy
          or email confirmation) to Finance within 5 business days.
  Step 5: Finance submits payment request to university accounts payable.
          Standard payment terms: Net 30 from invoice date.
  Step 6: Payment confirmation sent to vendor and copy archived in departmental
          financial records.

Late Payment Policy:
  Invoices not approved by the PI within 5 business days are escalated to the
  Department Chair. Repeated delays may result in vendor credit holds affecting
  the entire department.

================================================================
SECTION 5: DISPUTE & APPEALS PROCESS
================================================================

If a reimbursement claim is partially or fully denied:
  - Claimant receives written denial with specific reason(s) within 3 business days.
  - Claimant may appeal within 15 calendar days by submitting Form FIN-APPEAL-01
    with additional documentation supporting the claim.
  - Appeals are reviewed by the Finance Committee (Finance Officer + Dept. Chair)
    within 10 business days.
  - Finance Committee decision is final at the department level. University-level
    appeals may be filed with the Provost's Office within 30 days of the
    department's final decision.
