================================================================
        TEACHING ASSISTANT (TA) FORM PROCEDURES GUIDE
        Department of Computer Science & Engineering
        Academic Year 2024-2025
================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
This document outlines the complete procedures for submitting, processing, and
managing all Teaching Assistant-related forms within the department. All TAs,
faculty supervisors, and administrative staff must adhere to these procedures to
ensure timely processing of appointments, payments, and academic records.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: TA APPOINTMENT FORM (TAF-001)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
  The TA Appointment Form is the primary document used to officially assign a
  graduate or undergraduate student to a Teaching Assistant role for a given
  semester.

Required Fields:
  - Full legal name of the TA (as it appears on university records)
  - Student ID number (9-digit format: XXXXXXXXX)
  - Program of enrollment (MS, PhD, UG Honors)
  - Course number and section being supported (e.g., CS 5012-001)
  - Supervising faculty member's name and employee ID
  - Appointment type: Full TA (20 hrs/week) or Half TA (10 hrs/week)
  - Start date and end date of appointment
  - Stipend amount per month (must match department pay scale)
  - Tuition waiver status: Full / Partial / None

Submission Process:
  Step 1 — TA and supervising faculty both sign the printed form.
  Step 2 — Faculty supervisor submits the signed form to the Graduate Coordinator
            no later than 15 days before the semester start date.
  Step 3 — Graduate Coordinator reviews for completeness and forwards to the
            Registrar's Office within 3 business days.
  Step 4 — Registrar issues a confirmation email to the TA and faculty within
            5 business days of receiving the form.
  Step 5 — HR processes the appointment for payroll enrollment. TA must complete
            I-9 and W-4 forms if it is their first appointment.

Common Errors to Avoid:
  - Leaving the course section blank (causes processing delays of up to 2 weeks)
  - Mismatched stipend amounts not aligned with the current pay scale matrix
  - Missing second signature (form is returned immediately if unsigned)
  - Incorrect appointment dates spanning across two academic years

----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: TA DUTY MODIFICATION FORM (TAF-002)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
  Used when there is a change in a TA's assigned duties, hours, or course
  assignment after the original appointment has been confirmed.

When to Submit:
  - Change in weekly hours (e.g., from 10 hrs to 20 hrs)
  - Transfer of TA support from one course to another
  - Addition of a co-TA to the same course
  - Removal of TA duties mid-semester due to academic probation

Deadline:
  Must be submitted within 7 calendar days of the change taking effect.
  Retroactive modifications older than 30 days will not be accepted without a
  written appeal to the Department Chair.

Required Attachments:
  - Original TAF-001 confirmation email
  - Brief justification memo (max 300 words) signed by faculty supervisor
  - Updated schedule of duties (template available on department portal)

----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: TA PERFORMANCE EVALUATION FORM (TAF-003)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
  Submitted by the supervising faculty at the end of each semester to evaluate
  the TA's performance. Required for renewal of appointment in subsequent terms.

Evaluation Categories (each scored 1–5):
  - Attendance and punctuality at lab/recitation sessions
  - Quality of grading and feedback provided to students
  - Responsiveness to student queries (office hours, email)
  - Preparation and delivery of instructional content
  - Collaboration with course instructor
  - Overall professional conduct

Submission Deadline:
  No later than 7 days after the last day of final exams.

Consequences of Non-Submission:
  - TA's renewal eligibility is placed on hold.
  - Faculty member receives a formal reminder from Department Chair after 10 days.
  - If not submitted within 30 days post-semester, a default "satisfactory" rating
    is recorded, but a note is added to the faculty's administrative file.
