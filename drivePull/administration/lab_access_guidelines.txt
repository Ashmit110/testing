         Department of Computer Science & Engineering
         Engineering Building — Rooms 301, 305, 310, 315 (Grad Labs)

OVERVIEW
--------
This document specifies the policies, procedures, and safety requirements governing
access to all departmental computing and research labs. These guidelines apply to
all students, faculty, TAs, research assistants, and authorized visitors. Violations
may result in temporary or permanent revocation of lab access privileges.

================================================================
SECTION 1: CATEGORIES OF LAB ACCESS
================================================================

Level 1 — General Access (Undergraduate Computing Labs: Rooms 120, 122):
  Eligible users: All enrolled students of the department.
  Access hours:   Monday – Saturday: 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM
                  Sunday: 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM
  Access method:  University student ID card (RFID swipe at door).
  Supervision:    A lab monitor (student worker) is on duty during all open hours.

Level 2 — Restricted Access (Graduate Research Labs: Rooms 301, 305):
  Eligible users: MS and PhD students with an active research assignment + TAs
                  assigned to courses using these labs.
  Access hours:   24/7 card access.
  Access method:  Physical key + RFID card (dual authentication required after 9 PM).
  How to obtain:  Complete Form LA-01 (Lab Access Request), obtain PI/supervisor
                  signature, and submit to Room 204 (Admin Office).
  Processing time: 3 business days for standard requests.

Level 3 — High-Security Lab Access (GPU Cluster Room 310, Robotics Lab 315):
  Eligible users: PhD students working on approved grants + faculty + authorized
                  research staff.
  Access hours:   24/7, but solo access between 10 PM – 6 AM is prohibited.
                  At least 2 authorized persons must be present during these hours.
  Access method:  Biometric scanner (fingerprint) + RFID card.
  How to obtain:  Written request from faculty PI, submitted to Department Chair.
                  Background check required (university policy). Processing: 5–7
                  business days.
  Additional requirement: Mandatory lab safety orientation (2 hours) before
                  first access is granted.

================================================================
SECTION 2: LAB ACCESS REQUEST PROCEDURE (FORM LA-01)
================================================================

Who submits: The student or TA requiring access.

Required information on Form LA-01:
  - Full name and student/employee ID
  - Level of access requested (Level 1, 2, or 3)
  - Specific room number(s) requested
  - Purpose of access (coursework / research project / TA duties)
  - Expected access duration (semester / academic year / grant period)
  - Supervising faculty or PI name and email (auto-notification sent upon submission)
  - Agreement to abide by all lab policies (signature required)

Submission:
  Physical: Admin Office, Room 204
  Digital: dept-portal.university.edu/lab-access

Renewals:
  Access is semester-based for students. Renewals must be submitted 2 weeks before
  the semester ends. Access automatically expires 7 days after the semester end date
  unless renewed. PhD students on multi-year grants may request annual renewals.

Access Revocation:
  Lab access may be revoked at any time for policy violations, expiration of
  enrollment, or loss of TA/RA appointment. The student is notified by email
  from the Lab Administrator.

SECTION 3: LAB USE RULES & CONDUCT
================================================================

General Rules (All Levels):
  1. No food or beverages near computing equipment. Sealed water bottles are
     permitted at personal desks only.
  2. No personal equipment (external hard drives, USB hubs) may be connected to
     lab machines without written permission from the Lab Administrator.
  3. Installation of unauthorized software is strictly prohibited. All software
     requests must be submitted via Form SW-REQ-01 (see department portal).
  4. Users must log out of all sessions before leaving. Machines left logged in
     for more than 15 minutes will be automatically locked and the account flagged.
  5. Do not leave personal belongings unattended. The department is not responsible
     for lost or stolen items. Lockers are available near Room 120 (request key
     from admin office).
  6. Noise must be kept to a minimum. Group discussions requiring voice communication
     should be moved to the designated collaboration areas (Room 108 or 210).

Research Lab-Specific Rules (Level 2 & 3):
  1. Each user is assigned a specific workstation. Using another user's assigned
     machine requires written permission from the Lab Administrator.
  2. All research data must be backed up to the department's network storage
     (NAS) at least weekly. The department is not liable for data lost due to
     hardware failure on local machines.
  3. High-performance computing jobs (GPU cluster) must be submitted through the
     job queue system (SLURM). Direct use of GPUs without the scheduler is
     prohibited and will result in immediate suspension of access.
  4. Any hardware fault, damage, or security concern must be reported to the Lab
     Administrator within 24 hours via the issue tracker:
     dept-portal.university.edu/lab-issues

SECTION 4: SAFETY REGULATIONS
================================================================

Fire & Emergency:
  - Know the location of the nearest fire extinguisher (mounted at each lab entrance)
    and emergency exit (marked with green signage).
  - In case of fire, evacuate immediately and call 911 + Security (Ext. 0).
  - Do not use elevators during evacuation.
  - Muster point: Parking Lot B, east side of Engineering Building.

Electrical Safety:
  - Do not overload power strips. Each strip is rated for 15A maximum.
  - Report frayed cables, exposed wires, or burning smells immediately.
  - Portable space heaters are strictly prohibited in all labs.

Ergonomics:
  - Adjustable chairs and monitor stands are available in all labs. Contact the
    Lab Administrator if equipment adjustments are needed.
  - Recommended session breaks: 5 minutes every hour for eye strain reduction.

================================================================
SECTION 5: VISITOR & GUEST ACCESS
================================================================

External visitors (collaborators, visiting researchers) may be granted temporary
lab access under the following conditions:
  - Sponsoring faculty submits Form LA-GUEST-01 at least 3 business days in advance.
  - Visitor access is limited to Level 1 or Level 2 only.
  - Maximum duration: 2 weeks per visit. Extensions require Department Chair approval.
  - Visitor must sign the Lab Usage Agreement upon first entry.
  - Visitor must always be accompanied by a Level 2 or Level 3 authorized user
    in graduate research labs.

================================================================
SECTION 6: PRINTING & EQUIPMENT USAGE
================================================================

Printing:
  - Each student receives a printing quota of 200 pages/semester (black & white).
  - Color printing: 50 pages/semester. Additional pages: $0.05/page (black & white),
    $0.20/page (color). Charged to student account.
  - Large-format printing (posters): Submit request Form PRINT-01 at least
    72 hours in advance. Cost: $15/sq. ft. for gloss, $10/sq. ft. for matte.

Equipment Borrowing:
  - Cameras, tripods, Raspberry Pi kits, Arduino sets, and other portable equipment
    may be borrowed for up to 7 days. Submit Form EQUIP-BORROW-01.
  - Overdue equipment results in a hold on course registration.
  - Lost or damaged equipment must be reported immediately. Replacement cost
    will be charged to the student's university account.
