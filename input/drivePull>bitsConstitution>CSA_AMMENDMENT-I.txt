After the review by special body and having more than two third majority along with the CSA, the following
amendments have been submitted for approval:

* The part 2, Explanation 2, Article 10f of the constitution for President, Vice President and

Treasurer be amended to "The elected candidate should be in his/her the 3rd year for single degree students and 3
or 4" year for Dual degree students during their tenure. For the General Secretary be amended to "The elected
candidate should be in his/her 2» year for single or dual degree during their tenure.”

* The CGPA cut-off of candidates eligible for the post of CSA be amended to 5.50 for all posts of

CSA (President, Vice-President, Treasurer and General Secretary) from the current 6.00 as

mentioned in the constitution. This was proposed by the special body.

* In the clause 7(a), the word 'secret ballot’ should be removed from the clause regarding the

procedure of conducting elections.

* The clause 7(b) be amended to "Applications shall be invited by the Election Commission from

eligible candidates".

* The special Body also recommends the addition of a clause that the applications for

amendments to the election related issues should be entertained till a month before the

proposed/tentative election date (for that contact the C.E.C. or D.E.C.). The application for

amendments will be entertained in that month only if the director sir agrees to it.
