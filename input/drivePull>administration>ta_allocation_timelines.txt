================================================================
         TEACHING ASSISTANT ALLOCATION TIMELINES & PROCEDURES
         Department of Computer Science & Engineering
         Academic Year 2024-2025
================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
TA allocation is a structured, semester-by-semester process managed by the
Graduate Coordinator in consultation with course faculty and the Department Chair.
This document provides a detailed timeline of all allocation activities, deadlines,
and decision points for both Fall and Spring semesters.

================================================================
PART A: FALL SEMESTER ALLOCATION TIMELINE
================================================================

PHASE 1 — FACULTY NEEDS ASSESSMENT (March 1 – March 20)
---------------------------------------------------------
Action Items:
  - March 1:  Department Chair sends "TA Needs Request" email to all faculty
              teaching in Fall. Faculty must respond using Form FA-01.
  - March 10: Deadline for faculty to submit Form FA-01 indicating:
              * Course name and expected enrollment
              * Number of TA positions requested (Full or Half)
              * Preferred qualifications (e.g., must have completed CS 6010)
              * Special duties (grading-only vs. lab-support vs. recitation-lead)
  - March 15: Graduate Coordinator compiles all requests and cross-checks with
              available TA budget from department finance office.
  - March 20: Preliminary TA budget allocation draft is shared with Department Chair
              for approval.

PHASE 2 — TA APPLICANT POOL COMPILATION (March 21 – April 10)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Action Items:
  - March 21: TA Application portal opens for eligible students.
  - Eligible students: All PhD students in good standing + MS students with GPA ≥ 3.5
                       + Undergraduate Honors students (for select courses only)
  - Application requirements:
      * Statement of interest (max 500 words)
      * Unofficial transcript
      * List of up to 5 preferred courses with justification
      * Current advisor's email (auto-approval notification sent)
  - April 5:  TA Application portal closes. No late submissions accepted.
  - April 10: Graduate Coordinator finalizes the applicant pool and shares with
              relevant faculty for review.

PHASE 3 — MATCHING & ASSIGNMENT (April 11 – April 30)
------------------------------------------------------
Process:
  - Faculty review their shortlisted applicants and rank their top 3 candidates.
  - Graduate Coordinator uses a ranked matching algorithm to resolve conflicts
    where multiple faculty request the same student.
  - Priority rules for conflicts:
      1. PhD students in their 2nd year or beyond are prioritized over MS students.
      2. Students whose advisor is also the course instructor receive lower priority
         (to avoid conflicts of interest in grading).
      3. Students who have previously TAed the course successfully get priority.
  - April 25: Draft allocation list circulated to all faculty for 5-day review.
  - April 30: Final allocation list approved by Department Chair.

PHASE 4 — NOTIFICATION & ONBOARDING (May 1 – May 20)
-----------------------------------------------------
  - May 1:   Offer emails sent to all selected TAs from ta-admin@university.edu.
             TAs must accept or decline within 5 business days.
  - May 8:   Deadline for TA acceptance. Declined offers trigger re-allocation from
             waitlist.
  - May 10:  Appointment forms (TAF-001) generated and sent to both TA and faculty.
  - May 15:  Signed appointment forms due back to Graduate Coordinator.
  - May 20:  HR payroll enrollment initiated. TAs completing first appointment must
             attend mandatory onboarding session (see below).

MANDATORY TA ONBOARDING SESSION (held annually in late May):
  - Topics covered: Grading ethics, academic integrity policies, use of department
    LMS (Canvas), disability accommodation procedures, and emergency contacts.
  - Duration: 3 hours
  - Attendance is compulsory for first-time TAs. Returning TAs are encouraged to attend
    the 1-hour refresher version.

================================================================
PART B: SPRING SEMESTER ALLOCATION TIMELINE
================================================================

The Spring allocation process mirrors Fall, with the following adjusted dates:

  Phase 1 — Faculty Needs Assessment:  October 1 – October 20
  Phase 2 — TA Applicant Pool:         October 21 – November 10
  Phase 3 — Matching & Assignment:     November 11 – November 30
  Phase 4 — Notification & Onboarding: December 1 – December 20

Note: Due to the winter break, all onboarding for Spring TAs occurs in the first
week of January before classes begin.

================================================================
PART C: SPECIAL ALLOCATION SCENARIOS
================================================================

Mid-Semester Emergency Allocation:
  If a TA resigns or becomes ineligible mid-semester, the faculty supervisor must
  immediately notify the Graduate Coordinator. An emergency allocation request
  (Form EA-01) must be submitted within 48 hours. Resolution timeline: 7–10
  business days.

Summer Session TA Allocation:
  Summer TA needs are assessed in February. Only PhD students are eligible for
  Summer TA positions. The allocation is handled informally by the Graduate
  Coordinator with Department Chair approval, without a formal application process.

TA Overload Requests:
  A student may hold at most one Full TA position (20 hrs/week). Requests to hold
  two Half TA positions simultaneously must be approved by the student's academic
  advisor AND the Graduate Coordinator. Such overloads are granted only in
  exceptional circumstances.
