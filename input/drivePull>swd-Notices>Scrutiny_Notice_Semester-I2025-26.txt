NOTICE

Any student who wishes to apply for scrutiny of his/her grade for First Semester 2025-26
should follow the procedure given below:

1) Ifa student wants to apply for scrutiny of grades/ reports he/she may do so by filling in the
prescribed application form available in SWD website. If scrutiny of the grade is sought in
more than one course, separate application form for each course has to be used. The fee is
Rs. 100/- per course for Indian students and Rs. 350/- per_course for International
Students. This fee will be collected from the Mess Advance. The completed application
form has to be submitted in SWD office (B-102) on or before 22nd January 2026 by 4.30
PM.

2) Students need to submit all the documents related to the evaluation of the course, i.e.,
answer sheets of semester examinations, quizzes, etc., along with the application form.
In case of you are not having the documents, please mention in the application about it.

3) The scrutiny application will be sent to Associate Dean, AUGSD (Academic Undergraduates
Studies Division) for processing the case. The Associate Dean, AUGSD will interact with the
respective Instructor in-charge (I/C).

4) The I/C will submit observations on the application form to the Associate Dean, AUGSD
along with the following documents:
1) Complete evaluation records including marks of the course.
2) Histogram used for final grading.
3) Copy of the hand-out for the course.
4) Any other relevant records maintained by the I/C.

5) The Associate Dean, AUGSD prepares a summary of scrutiny appeals/ request of Instructor-
in-charge for scrutiny. This summary along with all the records is placed before the
Examination Sub Committee for the approval.

The intimation is given to the concerned student who applied for scrutiny after the approval
of Examination Sub Committee.

NOTE: Students currently in PS-II and Off campus thesis can mail the duly signed scanned
application of scrutiny form along with the other documents if any on swd@goa.bits-
pilani.ac.in. Separate mail will be sent to you regarding the payment for the same.

Associate Dean, SWD
