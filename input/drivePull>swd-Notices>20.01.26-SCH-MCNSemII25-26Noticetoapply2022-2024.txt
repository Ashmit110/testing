THE ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR AWARD OF SCHOLARSHIP

1. Scholarships are awarded to the student(s) for the standard course duration.

- The standard course duration for Single Degree is four years and for Dual Degree is five years.

2. There is no application for Merit scholarship. Merit scholarship will be given directly to the top
3% students of the respective batch.

3. Merit Scholarships will be awarded based on the CGPA obtained at the end of Semester | 25-26.

4. MCN scholarships will be awarded based on the GROSS TOTAL INCOME of the parent(s) and
CGPA obtained at the end of Semester | 25-26.

- The gross total income of Parent(s) from all sources should be less than the maximum
prescribed by the Scholarships Committee. The prescribed maximum limit for the previous
semester was Rs. 15,00,000/- per annum. The limit may vary from semester to semester; we
encourage interested students to apply for the scholarship.

- Aminimum CGPA of 6.00 is required for the award of scholarships.

- For 2024 batch onwards, a minimum CGPA of 8.00 is required for the award of MCN(100%)
scholarships.

5. No NC, RC, more than one E grade in the registered courses, case of indiscipline in Semester |,
2025-26.

6. Not availing other scholarships which stipulates that student will not receive any other
scholarship (for eg. INSPIRE/ KVPY/ Scholarships from Alumni Affairs Division like Bhushan
Bhatia etc.)

7. Apart from the above (refer point no. 6), students availing other scholarships/ financial assistance
will be considered for Merit/ MCN scholarship, provided they do not exceed the Tuition fee for
the respective semester/ academic year.

8. Any student availing of the fee benefit scheme through the Institute is not eligible.

9. Incase of any ambiguity, certificates in original should be produced by the student on demand.

10. This Merit/ MCN scholarship amount will be adjusted with the Tentative scholarship given with
Sem || 25-26 fees and the balance amount will be adjusted with Fees for the upcoming Semester.
