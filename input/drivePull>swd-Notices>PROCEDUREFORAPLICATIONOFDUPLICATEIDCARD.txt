PROCEDURE FOR APLICATION OF DUPLICATE ID CARD

1. Students need to visit physically to SWD office B-102 and apply for an ID Card and not via
email.

2.Students can apply for a duplicate ID card between 9.30 AM to 2.00 PM (Monday to Friday)
and between 9.30 AM to 11.30 AM (Saturday) in the SWD office.

3.ID cards will be issued on the same day only to the students who apply during the mentioned
period (as the list of applications needs to be routed through the Audio-Visual Unit for further
processing).

4.ID cards applied after the above-mentioned period will be issued on the next working day (in
case of Saturday's, ID cards will be issued on Monday).

5.Temporary id card slip will be issued to the students only if SWD is not able to issue the Id
card due to some technical issues only during office hours.

6.Students who have applied for Id card should collect it within the next 3 days from the SWD
B-102 office.

7.Students' friends/representatives will not be allowed to collect their id cards.
